FrontVeg V2

2026 · INRAE / IRHS, Angers, France

A geometry-aware, training-free framework for zero-shot plant trait segmentation in high-resolution images of trellised crops (vineyards, orchards). The pipeline automates foreground extraction using a novel Valley-Aware Depth Thresholding (VADT) algorithm applied to relative depth maps from Depth Anything V2. Tiled sliding-window inference with SAM3 produces fine-grained masks, which are assembled with a Graph-Based Mask Assembly (GMA) algorithm and filtered by a geometry-aware fusion step to suppress detections from neighbouring vegetation rows.

The framework handles leaf, fruit, and disease segmentation (black rot, downy mildew) across six geographic locations, improving foreground extraction Dice from 0.55 to 0.85 vs. the strongest RGB-only baseline. Available as a Python package on PyPI and as a Napari graphical plugin. Results presented at CVPPA / ECCV 2026.

FrontVeg (v1)

December 2024 – April 2025 · INRAE / IRHS, Angers, France

The first iteration of the FrontVeg pipeline: a Napari plugin for foreground vegetation segmentation in trellised crop images using RGB inputs. Users can run automatic inference and manually refine the generated mask within the Napari interface. Designed in the context of the European PHENET project, the plugin was co-developed with Herearii Metuarea (LARIS, Angers) under the supervision of Eric Duchêne, Morgane Roth, and David Rousseau.

Published to Napari Hub in April 2025. Superseded by FrontVeg V2 which adds depth-guided foreground detection, SAM3 integration, and multi-crop support.

DeepPhenoTree — Apple Phenology Detection Challenge

April 2026 – July 2026 · INRAE / IRHS

Co-organised a public machine learning benchmark on Codabench for detection of apple phenological stages in field images. The challenge is built around the DeepPhenoTree – Apple Edition dataset: 48,320 RGB images from four European Apple REFPOP orchard sites, with 241,600 expert annotations covering three BBCH phenological stage groups (Flowering, Fruitlet, Fruit).

The challenge features a Development Phase with live public leaderboard scoring and a Blind Evaluation Phase with a private test set. Participants predict YOLO-format bounding boxes evaluated automatically. The benchmark was presented at CVPPA / ECCV 2026. A companion dataset preprint is available on Research Square.

CV/ML-Based Fall Detection for the Elderly

June 2022 – May 2023 · INRIA Sophia Antipolis (STARS team)

End-to-end fall detection system targeting deployment on embedded devices for safe assisted living, developed as part of the SECUMAD project in collaboration with STARS, CoBTEK, and NODEUS. Falls are detected from camera footage alone — no wearable sensors required.

The approach adapts the UNIK skeleton-based action recognition framework. MediaPipe Pose extracts 17 body landmarks per frame in real time. Transfer learning from the Posetics dataset (142,000 clips) is followed by fine-tuning on PKU-MMD (1,076 untrimmed videos, 400 fall events, 66 subjects, 3 camera views). Post-processing determines the temporal start and end of detected falls in online video streams.
