Research Experience

  1. 2024 – Present

    Research Engineer — AgroEcoPhen

    INRAE / IRHS

    Angers, France

    Developing computer vision and machine learning tools for automated plant phenotyping in trellised agricultural crops (vineyards, orchards). Research focuses on zero-shot segmentation of plant organs and disease symptoms from high-resolution field images, without requiring task-specific model retraining.

    Key outputs include FrontVeg and FrontVeg V2 (PyPI / Napari), a CVPPA / ECCV 2026 workshop paper, a SoftwareX paper under review, co-organisation of the DeepPhenoTree data challenge on Codabench, and staff training courses on deep learning and software engineering practices.

    Supervisor: Prof. David Rousseau — University of Angers

    Python PyTorch SAM Depth Estimation Plant Phenotyping

  2. June 2022 – May 2023

    Research Engineer

    INRIA Sophia Antipolis  ·  STARS Team

    Nice, France

    Designed and implemented a skeleton-based fall detection system for elderly people as part of the SECUMAD project, a collaboration between STARS, CoBTEK and NODEUS. The camera-only approach uses pose estimation to avoid the need for wearable sensors, targeting deployment on embedded devices.

    Adapted the UNIK spatio-temporal action recognition framework with MediaPipe Pose (17 body landmarks), applied transfer learning from the Posetics dataset, and fine-tuned on PKU-MMD (1,076 untrimmed videos, 400 fall events). Developed temporal localisation post-processing for online video streams.

    Supervisor: Prof. François Brémond — INRIA

    Python PyTorch MediaPipe Raspberry Pi Action Recognition
