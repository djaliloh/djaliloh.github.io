Foreground-Aware Zero-Shot Plant Trait Segmentation in Trellised Crops Using Side-View Monocular RGB Images

Abdoul Djalil Ousseini Hamza, Herearii Metuarea, Corentin Lothodé, Morgane Roth, Eric Duchêne, Lionel Ley, David Allétru, David Marc Rousseau

11th Workshop on Computer Vision in Plant Phenotyping and Agriculture (CVPPA), ECCV 2026

Abstract

Zero-shot plant phenotyping in trellised crops remains challenging because field images simultaneously capture multiple vegetation rows with similar visual appearances. We propose a geometry-aware, training-free framework combining monocular depth estimation (Depth Anything V2) with a novel Valley-Aware Depth Thresholding (VADT) algorithm to automatically isolate the foreground vegetation row. Tiled zero-shot segmentation with SAM3, Graph-Based Mask Assembly, and geometry-aware fusion complete the pipeline. Evaluated on 158 RGB images with ~10,000 manual annotations across vineyards, apple orchards, and apricot orchards. VADT improves foreground extraction Dice from 0.55 to 0.85 vs. the strongest RGB-only baseline.

FrontVeg V2: An Open-Source, Training-Free Software Framework for Foreground-Aware Zero-Shot Segmentation of Plant Traits in High-Resolution Images of Trellised Crops

Abdoul Djalil Ousseini Hamza, Herearii Metuarea, Corentin Lothodé, Morgane Roth, Eric Duchêne, Lionel Ley, David Allétru, David Marc Rousseau

SoftwareX, Elsevier — submitted 2026, under review. INRAE / IRHS.

Abstract

FrontVeg V2 is an open-source, training-free software framework for foreground-aware zero-shot segmentation of plant traits in high-resolution images of trellised crops. The pipeline combines monocular depth estimation, automatic foreground extraction via Valley-Aware Depth Thresholding, tiled zero-shot segmentation, Graph-Based Mask Assembly, and geometry-aware fusion. The current implementation integrates Depth Anything V2 and SAM3, accessible through both command-line batch processing and a Napari graphical interface. FrontVeg V2 provides a reusable framework for multi-crop, multi-trait digital phenotyping without task-specific model retraining.

DeepPhenoTree – Apple Edition: A Multi-Site Apple Phenology RGB Annotated Dataset with Deep Learning Baseline Models

Herearii Metuarea, Abdoul Djalil Ousseini Hamza, Walter Guerra, Francesca Zuffa, Francesco Panzeri, Andrea Patocchi, Lidia Lozano, Shauny Hoye, François Laurens, Jeremy Labrosse, Pejman Rasti, David Rousseau

Preprint. Research Square. DOI: 10.21203/rs.3.rs-8977752/v1

Abstract

We introduce DeepPhenoTree – Apple Edition, a multi-site, multi-variety RGB image dataset for detection of key phenological stages in apple trees. The dataset comprises 48,320 time-stamped RGB images from four European orchards of the Apple REFPOP consortium. A curated subset of 808 representative images contains 241,600 expert annotations covering developmental stages from dormant bud to fruit maturity, following the BBCH scale. Baseline deep learning experiments are provided to assess model generalisation across locations.

Fall Detection in Untrimmed Videos

Abdoul Djalil Ousseini Hamza, Yoann Torrado, Jose Francisco Saray, François Brémond

INRIA Activity Report — STARS Team, Sophia Antipolis, 2022–2023. Part of the SECUMAD project, in collaboration with STARS, CoBTEK, and NODEUS.

Abstract

We adapt the UNIK skeleton-based activity recognition framework to detect falls in untrimmed videos, without requiring wearable sensors. MediaPipe Pose is used to extract 17 body landmarks per frame, enabling transfer learning from the Posetics dataset. Fine-tuning on PKU-MMD (1,076 long videos, 400 fall events, 66 subjects) yields an end-to-end system capable of temporal localisation of fall events in online video streams, targeting deployment on Raspberry Pi embedded devices.
